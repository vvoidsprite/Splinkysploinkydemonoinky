# The Sealed Manor (working title)

A dark fantasy story RPG about a priestess raised to guard a seal, and the sealed being she loves. This glossary fixes the vocabulary of the story world. All proper names below are **placeholders** and can be renamed.

## Language

### People

**Maren**:
The protagonist, a fixed female character. A priestess of the Lumen Order, raised from birth to inherit the duty of keeping the Seal closed.
_Avoid_: the player character, the heroine

**Lychkin**:
The bloodline whose members are born to hold the Seal. Maren is its heir, and her parent is its current holder. (Placeholder name; a fantasy term native to the world is wanted.)
_Avoid_: Warden-line, royal line, chosen family

**Edrin**:
A sincere young Lumen Order acolyte assigned to look after Maren at the manor, and her human helper. His kindness is genuine, and he sides with the Order in every ending. (Placeholder name.)
_Avoid_: sidekick, companion

**Warden**:
A human enforcer of the Lumen Order who hunts those who stray from doctrine or approach the Seal.
_Avoid_: Censor, guard, inquisitor

**Mark**:
The sign on a Lychkin heir's body that is part of the Seal itself, acting as both lock and key.
_Avoid_: birthmark, curse

### Faiths

**Lumen Order**:
The invented church that raised Maren and built its authority over the Seal.
_Avoid_: the church, Christianity

**Old Faith**:
The older belief the Lumen Order overwrote. Its fragments survive in Order scripture, inverted into sins and prohibitions. Its followers' own name for it is not yet decided.
_Avoid_: pagan religion, cult

### The Sealed One

**Caldris**:
The being bound beneath the cathedral, whose body and the Seal are one. The Lumen Order calls it a *demon*; the Old Faith may hold it to be a guardian or god. Narration outside the Order's voice never uses the word "demon" for it unchallenged.
_Avoid_: the demon (outside Order dialogue), the monster, the beast

**Seal**:
The binding that holds Caldris, fused with Caldris's body beneath the cathedral and kept shut by the Lychkin heir's devotion together with the Rites of Renewal. Genuine affection for Caldris frays it.
_Avoid_: lock, prison, ward

**Rite of Renewal**:
A ritual the Lychkin heir must perform on a schedule to keep the Seal strong. Maren has not performed the latest one.
_Avoid_: ceremony, prayer, maintenance

**Fragment**:
A small piece of Caldris, shaped as a shadow animal such as a dog or a crow, that slips through the weakened Seal to help and protect Maren. Fragments always take the shape of real animals.
_Avoid_: shadow-self, familiar, ghost, spirit

**Save crow**:
A Fragment in crow form that appears where the game can be saved. It offers to "keep this moment" for Maren, speaks as if it has always known her, and never explains saving. In Maren's room it vanishes once she gets out, leaving a feather. (Placeholder name.)
_Avoid_: save point, checkpoint

**Bound**:
Sentient Old Faith creatures imprisoned by the Order and loyal to Caldris. They are non-existent, fantastical creatures (in the spirit of Stardew Valley's Junimos), never real animals, so they cannot be mistaken for Fragments. Freeing one is an optional, well-hidden act. (Placeholder name; a better one is wanted.)
_Avoid_: demons (outside Order dialogue), spirits, prisoners

**Warped**:
Mindless things twisted by the Seal's leak, such as statues and remains, that chase Maren. They grow stranger the deeper she goes. They are mostly present for gameplay.
_Avoid_: monsters, enemies, undead

**Blessings**:
The magic Vareth gains from Caldris's imprisonment, which the Lumen Order presents as gifts from its gods.
_Avoid_: miracles, powers

**Unbinding**:
The ritual that frees Caldris fully from the Seal. This is the "resurrection" Maren seeks.
_Avoid_: resurrection (in game text), summoning, awakening

**Verse**:
The spoken formula that performs the Unbinding. It exists as three pieces, each held by a Bound creature. Speaking it becomes a choice for Maren only once all three pieces are found. The version the Bound remember has drifted over centuries and contains a line implying Maren will betray Caldris; the original wording is carved in the secret crypt areas.
_Avoid_: spell, incantation, chant

### Places

**Hollowmere Manor**:
The residence of the Lumen Order's priests and the seat of the Lychkin, standing beside the cathedral on the same grounds. Maren was brought here as a child to learn her duty and first met Caldris.
_Avoid_: the house, the mansion

**Cathedral of the Pale Lamp**:
The Lumen Order's temple, built over the Seal on the manor's grounds, beside Hollowmere Manor.
_Avoid_: the church building, the temple

**Vareth**:
The distant city whose power and authority rest on the Seal. It is mentioned in the world but is not a playable area.
_Avoid_: the capital, the city
