# Creative direction

The owner is a 2D artist and writer who loves books and wants to start in game development. They want story and lore to lead.

Reference games (tone, not mechanics to copy): Dark Souls, Fear & Hunger, The Witch's House, Mad Father, Undertale.

What they share: lore told through the world and small details rather than exposition, dark or unsettling atmosphere, and choices or discoveries that reward curiosity.

When proposing features, lean toward storytelling and atmosphere (dialogue, environmental storytelling, lore items, branching outcomes) and toward 2D art-friendly solutions the owner can make assets for themselves.
